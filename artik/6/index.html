So, has my perspective on DeFiLlama completely changed? Yes, it has. It’s no longer just a list of who’s got the most crypto, but a valuable tool for navigating the complex and sometimes bewildering world of decentralized finance. It's a starting point for research, not a conclusion. And that, I think, is a much more nuanced and interesting way to look at things. After all, even the best-ranked protocol can have hidden flaws, and a seemingly small protocol might be the next big thing. Finding those hidden gems? That’s the real adventure.

This journey of understanding DefiLlama's methodology made me realize how much work still needs to be done to create a truly comprehensive, insightful and nuanced view of DeFi’s ever-evolving landscape. It's not as simple as a number, is it?
