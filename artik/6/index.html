Unique Routing Algorithms Used by DefiLlama Swap

DefiLlama Swap does not rely on simple brute-force comparisons; its routing engine utilizes a unique combination of algorithms designed for speed and optimal *net* output for the user, differentiating it from competitors.

The core algorithm performs a **multi-variable optimization** that extends beyond the raw token price. It simultaneously factors in four main components: the raw exchange rate, the depth of liquidity (to minimize slippage), the DEX protocol fee, and the anticipated network gas cost.

Unlike some protocols that may only calculate simple two- or three-hop paths, DefiLlama Swap employs advanced graph theory to find complex, multi-segment routes. This means it can split a single order into several sub-orders, routing each one to a different DEX and pool to capture the absolute best rates available across the ecosystem.

A key distinguishing feature is the routing of trades through **other aggregators** (e.g., querying 1inch's best route against its own calculations), a meta-aggregation approach that guarantees the user gets the best price offered anywhere in the aggregated DeFi market.

For cross-chain swaps, the algorithm integrates **bridge aggregation logic**. It analyzes the security, liquidity, and fee structure of various cross-chain bridges (like Synapse or Stargate) and treats the entire cross-chain transfer as part of the overall pathfinding equation.

The algorithm is also highly effective in handling thin liquidity. When faced with an illiquid token pair, it intelligently utilizes intermediate, highly liquid stablecoins or major tokens (like ETH or USDT) to create a multi-hop path that minimizes price impact.

Furthermore, the entire routing process is informed by the massive, real-time, **unbiased data feeds** of the parent DefiLlama analytics platform, ensuring the calculations are based on the most accurate and independently verified market data possible.

In essence, the unique algorithmic design ensures the trader receives the highest possible token output, treating the entire decentralized market as a single source of optimal liquidity.
