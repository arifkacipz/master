Instead of every single request hitting your precious servers directly, Squidrouter intercepts them. If it's already cached a response, it serves it up lightning-fast. If not, it fetches it, caches it, and then serves it to everyone else who asks for the same thing. Suddenly, that mountain of requests is getting significantly trimmed down – your servers are breathing a sigh of relief, and your users are experiencing noticeably snappier load times.

Now, I'm no expert. I still get a little lost in the weeds when I delve into the more advanced configuration options (those nested curly braces… shudders).
