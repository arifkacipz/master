How Liquidity Depth Affects DefiLlama Swap Arbitrage

Liquidity depth is the **most critical factor** determining the viability and profitability of any arbitrage or swap executed through DefiLlama Swap. Arbitrage profit is always relative to liquidity depth.

Arbitrage requires sufficient **liquidity depth** in the pools to execute the buy and sell orders. In shallow pools, the trader's buy order will immediately push the price up (high slippage), eliminating the arbitrage spread and making the trade unprofitable.

DefiLlama Swap’s algorithm uses liquidity depth analysis to determine the **Maximal Executable Arbitrage**. It ensures that the profit captured for the user through the best-price route is not wiped out by the slippage caused by the order size.

For large trades, the core function of the aggregator is to **synthesize depth**. By splitting the order across multiple pools, including concentrated liquidity pools, it maximizes the use of available capital while minimizing the price impact that would destroy the arbitrage margin.

For **cross-chain arbitrage**, liquidity depth is doubly important: the bridge itself must have sufficient liquidity to handle the asset transfer, or the entire trade will stall or fail, turning a potential profit into a loss.

DefiLlama's data-centric approach ensures that routing decisions are always grounded in the reality of **verifiable liquidity depth**, optimizing for profitability where the capital actually exists.
