Chasing Unicorns and APRs: Modeling Compound Interest with DeFi Llama

It tracks everything from total value locked (TVL) to, crucially for our purposes, the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) offered by various DeFi protocols. The APR is the promise, the sourdough starter's potential. Compound interest, the magic of interest earning interest, is the hoped-for loaf of perfectly risen bread. But can we really model this magical growth accurately? That’s what I set out to explore.

My initial approach was naive, bordering on optimistic. I envisioned a simple spreadsheet, a few formulas, and bam, a perfect prediction of my future DeFi riches. After some trial and error (and a few existential crises fueled by late-night coding sessions), I settled on a relatively simple approach: a Monte Carlo simulation. This essentially runs the compound interest calculation many times, each time using a slightly different (and randomly selected) APR based on historical data. This gives you a range of possible outcomes, rather than a single, potentially misleading prediction. It's less precise, but it acknowledges the inherent uncertainty.
