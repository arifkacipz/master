In practice… well, let's just say it's less like a perfectly orchestrated symphony and more like a jazz improv session where the musicians haven't quite rehearsed.

Looking at DefiLlama, you see a range of these algorithmic stablecoins, some thriving, some… not so much. It’s a stark reminder that the “algorithmic” part isn't some magic bullet.
