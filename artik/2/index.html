But its real magic – and its potential source of endless head-scratching – lies in its routing logic. This is where the path your network traffic takes gets defined.

In reality, it's more like trying to herd cats while simultaneously juggling chainsaws. One of the things I found most confusing (and still kind of am, to be honest) is the order of operations. SquidProxy evaluates these rules in a specific sequence.
