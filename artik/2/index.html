It's almost like trying to count the number of people in a city by looking at both the number of houses and the number of people on the streets at a given time – you'll likely get some overlap.

So, what's the takeaway? Is comparing TVL and CEX volumes a fruitless endeavor? Not entirely. While they don’t paint a completely comparable picture, each metric offers valuable insights. TVL provides a sense of the potential within DeFi, highlighting growing interest and locked capital. CEX volumes, meanwhile, reveal the immediate activity and liquidity in the market.
