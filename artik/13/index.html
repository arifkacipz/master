So, let’s talk security prompts for your SquidRouter. It’s not rocket science, but it’s definitely not something you should ignore. Not some slacker who leaves the gate wide open for any digital rogue to waltz in.

The Nitty Gritty (and a few gentle nudges):

* Strong Passwords: This seems obvious, but let's be real, how many of us have passwords that are essentially variations of "password123"? I’m guilty as charged. Let's aim for something stronger than that. Think long, complex, and ideally, use a password manager. Yes, I know, another thing to manage, but trust me, it's worth the effort. Your future self will thank you. And so will your precious cat videos.

* Regular Updates: This is crucial. Think of software updates as security patches. They're like tiny little digital knights swooping in to fix vulnerabilities before they become gaping holes in your defenses. Neglecting updates is like leaving your front door unlocked – inviting trouble.

Limiting access to authorized devices significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized intrusions. It’s about creating a digital moat around your most valuable assets.

* HTTPS Everywhere: This one's a no-brainer. HTTPS encrypts your communication, making it much harder for prying eyes to intercept your data. It’s like sending your secrets in a locked box rather than on a postcard.

Now, I know what you’re thinking: "This is all a bit overwhelming." And you know what? You’re right. It can be. But it doesn’t have to be a daunting mountain to climb. Start small. Focus on one or two of these prompts and build from there. It's a journey, not a sprint.
